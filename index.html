About RAD

Who we are

Portrait coming soon

Bobby Anderson · Founder, This is RAD

We make complex digital products feel simple.

RAD is the independent design practice of Bobby Anderson — a senior product designer with over 20 years of experience across fintech, banking, SaaS, health tech and consumer products. The work sits at the intersection of UX, UI and product strategy, where the real design decisions happen.

It's not just about how things look. It's about how they work, why they exist, and whether they're solving the right problem — with founders and product teams who treat design as a strategic function, not a finishing step.
